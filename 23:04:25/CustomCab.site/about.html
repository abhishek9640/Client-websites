About CustomCab

Our story, our passion, our commitment to quality

Our Brand Story

Established in 2005, CustomCab emerged with one obvious purpose: to build outstanding custom cabinetry that creates spaces and surpasses client expectations. A humble workshop that initially began has expanded to become a top cabinet design and installation firm, revered by homeowners all over the nation.

Our founder, John Thompson, merged his background in traditional woodworking with contemporary design concepts to develop a distinctive style of cabinet making that respects artisanal skill while welcoming innovation.
